The maison

A small studio, a slow practice

Every piece in the collection begins on a workbench in Ghent. We work in small batches with natural materials — Belgian flax, oak, wool, and stoneware — and we would rather make one thing well than a hundred things quickly.

The result is a home full of objects with a little more soul, and a little more life left in them.
